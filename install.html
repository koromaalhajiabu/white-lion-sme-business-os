WHITE LION TECH SOLUTIONS

Install White Lion SME

Add White Lion SME to your phone's home screen. It opens in its own app window. Your business records remain in the app's local storage and sync when connected.

Open the business system
Android (Chrome):
1. Tap Install White Lion SME if the button is available.
2. If not, open Chrome menu ⋮ and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
3. Confirm, then open White Lion SME from your home screen or app list.

iPhone (Safari):
1. Open this page in Safari.
2. Tap the Share icon.
3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

If it says already installed: open the existing White Lion SME icon. Do not create another shortcut.
