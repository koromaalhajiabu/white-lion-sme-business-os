Frequently asked questions

What is White Lion SME Business OS?

It is a business operations platform from White Lion Tech Solutions. Depending on the modules enabled for your workspace, it can help you manage sales, products and inventory, purchases, customers, suppliers, expenses, accounting records, employees and branch operations.

What types of businesses can use it?

The platform is designed for different small and medium-sized businesses, including retail shops, supermarkets, pharmacies, hardware businesses, wholesalers and other service or trading businesses. Available modules depend on your workspace configuration.

Can I use it on an Android phone or computer?

Yes. The application is designed to work in a compatible modern web browser on Android phones, tablets and desktop computers. Supported devices may also offer an install option for convenient access.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Some supported operations can be saved locally while offline and synchronised when your device reconnects. Not every screen or feature is available offline. For important transactions, confirm that synchronisation has completed before relying on the records in another device.

Can I manage more than one branch?

Yes. The platform supports branch operations, including branch membership and stock-transfer workflows where those features are enabled. Access depends on the permissions assigned by your business owner or administrator.

Can my employees use the system?

Yes. A business owner or authorised administrator can add team members and assign access according to their responsibilities. Give each employee only the permissions needed for their work.

Can another business see my records?

Business workspaces are separated through account membership and database access controls. Do not share your login credentials. Report any access that you do not recognise through an official White Lion support channel.

Who can see information within my business?

Access within a workspace is governed by the roles and permissions assigned to its members. Business owners and authorised administrators should review team access regularly, especially when an employee changes role or leaves.

How do I get training?

Ask White Lion Tech Solutions through the official support channel provided with your account or subscription information. Tell us which business modules you want help learning.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Use another compatible device to access your account and sign in with your authorised credentials. If you suspect someone else can access your account, contact support promptly and change your password through the approved account process.

Are my records automatically backed up and guaranteed recoverable?

The platform uses cloud storage and synchronisation for supported records. However, customers should not assume that every unsynchronised offline change is already in the cloud or that recovery is guaranteed. Confirm sync status and contact support if records appear to be missing.

Can I change my subscription?

Available upgrades or plan changes depend on the plans and billing options shown in your Plans & Billing panel. Review the displayed terms before confirming a change.

What happens when my subscription expires?

Subscription access is governed by the plan and billing status shown in your account. Check Plans & Billing and renew before the displayed expiry date if you want to avoid an interruption to subscription-controlled features.

Getting started with your business

  1. Create your account. Register and sign in using your own email address and a strong password.
  2. Create or open your business workspace. Enter accurate business information and select the appropriate business type or industry setup.
  3. Review your enabled modules. Check which operational features are available to your workspace and subscription.
  4. Set up your team. Add employees only when needed and assign permissions appropriate to their roles.
  5. Configure your products and stock. Enter product details and opening quantities carefully. Check units, costs and selling prices before recording transactions.
  6. Set up branches if required. Create branches and assign branch members with the correct access.
  7. Record a small test transaction. Learn the sales or purchasing workflow before entering large volumes of live business records.
  8. Check synchronisation. When working offline, reconnect and confirm that supported records have synchronised successfully.
  9. Review your reports. Compare reports against your source records and investigate differences before using figures for business decisions.
Important: Take care when entering opening balances, stock quantities and financial transactions. If you are unsure how to record an adjustment, ask for guidance before posting it.

Subscription payments using Orange Money

  1. Sign in and open Plans & Billing.
  2. Choose the subscription plan and request an invoice.
  3. Read the invoice and confirm the exact amount and currency due.
  4. Send the exact invoiced amount using Orange Money to 075075818.
  5. Keep the completed transaction ID or reference from Orange Money.
  6. Return to the payment form, select Orange Money · 075075818, and submit the transaction reference and invoiced amount.
  7. Wait while White Lion Tech Solutions manually checks the payment. Your subscription is activated only after the payment is verified and approved.
Protect your money: Do not transfer money without first requesting an invoice. Submit a transaction reference only after a completed transfer. A submitted reference is not proof that funds have been received and does not activate a subscription automatically. Never share your Orange Money PIN or account password.

If you have paid but your subscription is not yet active, keep your invoice number and transaction reference and contact White Lion Tech Solutions through the official support channel.

Support and complaints policy

How to request help

Contact White Lion Tech Solutions through the official support contact supplied with your account, invoice or subscription communication. Include:

  • Your business or workspace name.
  • The email address used to sign in (never your password).
  • The page or feature you were using.
  • A clear description of what happened and the steps that led to it.
  • The approximate date and time of the issue.
  • A screenshot with passwords, PINs, personal financial details and other sensitive information hidden.

Payment enquiries

For a payment enquiry, provide the invoice number, amount, payment provider and transaction reference. Do not send your Orange Money PIN, one-time security codes or password. Payment verification is performed manually against the actual payment received.

How complaints are handled

  1. We review the information you provide and may ask for relevant details needed to investigate.
  2. We assess whether the issue relates to account access, configuration, a technical fault, billing or user training.
  3. We provide an update or explain the next action once we have sufficient information.
  4. If the issue cannot be resolved immediately, we will explain what is still being investigated rather than promise an unverified resolution time.

Refunds and cancellations

For cancellation or refund enquiries, contact White Lion Tech Solutions with your business name, invoice number and reason for the request. Each request must be reviewed against the applicable subscription terms and the payment status. This guide does not promise an automatic refund or a particular refund timeframe.

Security and responsible use

  • Never share your password, Orange Money PIN or one-time security code with anyone.
  • Do not give employees shared owner credentials; use individual accounts and appropriate permissions.
  • Report suspected unauthorised access promptly.
  • Do not submit false payment references or use another person's account without permission.
  • Keep your device and browser updated and protect devices used to access business information.
Our service commitment: White Lion Tech Solutions aims to communicate respectfully, handle customer information carefully, investigate reported issues and give honest updates. We will not claim that a payment, backup, feature or repair is complete until it has been checked.

This help centre explains the current customer-support process. Where a separate signed agreement or published subscription terms apply, those terms govern the relevant commercial arrangement.
